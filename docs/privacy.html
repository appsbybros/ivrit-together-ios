Your learning stays yours.

Privacy policy for Ivrit Together for iPhone and iPad, bundle ID com.appsbybros.ivrittogether. Effective 10 October 2026. Developer: Apps by Bros.

Information on your device

The app stores your interface language and learning preferences, completed lessons, practice results, review dates, saved phrases and practice-day history in the app's private storage on your device. These records support your learning and are not sent to us.

No account or tracking

The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, cloud backend or in-app purchase. It contains no network code of its own and does not ask for access to your microphone, contacts, location, photos or camera. It does not record your voice and does not track you across apps or websites.

Speech and device services

Listening uses Apple's speech synthesis on your device with an installed Hebrew voice. The lesson text you choose to hear is passed to that on-device voice. If no Hebrew voice is installed, Voice settings opens the app's page in the Settings app; downloading and managing voices is handled by iOS and iPadOS, where Apple's own terms and privacy practices apply.

Rate, share and feedback

Rate this app opens the App Store or Apple's review prompt. Share this app opens the system share sheet with the app's name and App Store link; you choose whether and where to send it. Send feedback opens your email app with a message to us; nothing is sent until you send it.

Backups and deletion

Depending on your settings, iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer may include the app's local data. You control those settings. Use Settings → Reset learning progress to remove learning history and saved phrases; this keeps your interface preferences. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device; backup retention is controlled by your backup provider.

Support

If you contact appsbybros.dev@gmail.com, we receive the message and the information you choose to send. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is used to respond to your request; it is separate from the app's offline learning records. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence we hold, subject to any required retention.

Changes

If future versions introduce additional data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated before those features are released.
