A little Hebrew. A new beginning.

Order a coffee. Ask someone to speak more slowly. Find your bus stop. Ivrit Together helps adult beginners practise the Hebrew they can use in everyday life in Israel.

• 18 practical lessons, with 108 words and phrases.
• Learn with clear Hebrew, optional vowel marks and pronunciation guides.
• Listen at a normal or slower pace with a Hebrew voice on your iPhone or iPad.
• Choose meanings, build sentences and match pairs.
• Revisit phrases through spaced review and keep a personal phrasebook.
• Explore all 22 letters, including the five final letter forms.
• Choose masculine or feminine examples where available.
• Learn in English, Russian, Ukrainian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Amharic, German or Hebrew.

A calm, adult design with no countdowns or lost lives. Progress stays on your device. No account, ads, subscription or in-app purchase.

Lessons and practice work offline. Audio uses a Hebrew voice installed on your device; if none is available, you can download one in your device's Accessibility settings. Without a voice, you can still read and practise. This introductory course complements real conversation and Hebrew classes; it does not assess pronunciation or certify fluency.
